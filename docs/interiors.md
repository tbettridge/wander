# Contextual interiors

Buildings now derive an interior architecture descriptor without consuming the exterior generator's random sequence. Ground-room uses follow the entrance: public/common/work spaces face the front; storage and private rooms sit behind them. Upper floors contain bedrooms, offices, or storage according to the building program. Some domestic buildings and barns receive partial loft decks. Floors leave genuine stair holes; stairs, landings, railings, partitions, portal links, and walkable claims share the same descriptor.

`interiorcatalog.mjs` defines 30 parametric furniture and decoration families. `interiorplan.mjs` uses bounded constraint propagation: filter candidate domains against circulation and occupied space, place required furnishings first, then collapse the smallest remaining domain. Large rooms receive a limited number of extra furnishings; seating can align with tables. Narrow beds provide a required-bed fallback. Optional omissions are recorded in diagnostics. This is WFC-inspired placement without unbounded search or timing-dependent retries.

Household identity supplies a consistent palette. Seeded textiles, wear, clutter order, board seams, wall hangings, and supported books, vessels, plants, lamps, blankets, and tools add variation. Complete access paths, doorway lanes, stair shafts, and landings remain reserved. Attached solid masses are excluded from placement. Furniture collision dimensions stay independent of render detail.

## Streaming and lighting

The settlement shell, structural floors/stairs, walkable claims, and collision metadata remain resident with the settlement. Detailed room geometry streams separately. `InteriorStream` predicts entrance intent over 1.7 seconds from player velocity, prepares within 15 m, maintains window previews within 26 m, and enables nearby/current-floor decoration within 8 m. Lofts remain visible from below. There are at most two queued requests, one room upload per frame, 24 admitted rooms, and 24 MiB of retained vertex attributes. Rooms hide on departure, remain warm for 15 seconds, then release geometry and materials; cold rooms are evicted sooner to make space. Upload admission shares the settlement's 2 ms assembly allowance. A single upload or fallback preparation cannot be interrupted mid-operation.

The module worker produces transferable position/normal/color arrays. Each furnished room uses at most two meshes, rather than one object per furnishing part. Worker failure falls back to one room preparation at a time. Small decoration is suppressed for exterior XR previews. Diagnostics are available at `__wander.settlements.interiors.metrics` and in `worldState.metrics.interior`.

Vertex colors bake simple contact/directional shading. The room material combines normal sun/sky lighting with attenuation from four nearby real window openings and up to two warm fixture contributions. Fixture animation and day/night intensity are shader uniforms. It adds no shadow maps, global Three.js lights, or render targets. Inner wall/ceiling faces receive room lighting; their original windows and doors remain holes. Outdoor fog is disabled on room surfaces while the exterior keeps its normal atmosphere. Window blur and exterior simplification based on glass blur are deferred. The exterior scene is not aggressively culled on entry.

## Residents and rollback

NPCs route through the same portals and stair ramps as players. Furniture use spots preserve their canonical root on the floor while the visual skeleton sits on a seat or lies on a mattress. Shared beds have separate body slots; furniture slots are exclusive, with an unposed free-floor fallback for crowded households. Window-watching spots exclude furniture and attached wings. Steering neighbours are separated by floor height. Public shared-world snapshots carry bounded furniture poses for visiting players.

`worldState.features.interiorsEnabled = false` hides furnishings, disables their collision, and releases furniture poses. Structural floors and stairs remain usable. `interiorDecorationsEnabled = false` removes small render details while preserving the layout and furniture collision. Both flags participate in feature normalization.

## Validation

Run the pure regressions with `node --test --test-concurrency=4 tests/*.mjs`. The completed suite reports 1,047 passes, zero failures, and six existing TODOs. Interior tests cover 1,400 layouts across all 14 programs, determinism, required usable beds, circulation, stair holes/headroom, bidirectional supported routes, live NPC steering, finite bounded worker buffers, indoor venue clearance, admission limits, entrance momentum, collision rollback, and furniture pose reset. Shared-world tests cover bounded pose serialization. An existing water-cache LRU test is timestamp-sensitive under heavy parallel load; its isolated rerun and the controlled full run passed.

`npm run playtest:interiors -- --game` runs optional Chrome/Playwright checks. Install Playwright or set `WANDER_PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` to its `index.mjs`; `WANDER_CHROME_PATH` can choose a Chrome binary. The runner checks every program, worker fallback, r185/r165 shaders, warm retention/eviction/re-entry, dense-street bounds, and actual player stair physics with pointer lock. It writes screenshots and a JSON report to a temporary directory. `interior-lab.html` provides an interactive preview using the production builders, worker, floor providers, and collision index. Arrow keys move and A/D turn.

On the local Chrome run, the dense fixture admitted 24 rooms with 2,153,952 bytes of vertex attributes; assembly p95 was about 0.3 ms and the largest observed room upload was about 0.7 ms. A worker-disabled house prepared/uploaded a room in about 1.9 ms. The warm house's measured CPU frame p95 was about 1.2 ms with furnishings and 1.0 ms without. These are local CPU submission measurements, including an unmerged lab shell; they do not establish GPU cost or a Quest frame-time guarantee. The real game climbed and descended the inn's 3.15 m storey using all 32 route waypoints in each direction, without teleporting during traversal. Domestic and barn lofts were separately rendered and visually checked. Hardware XR profiling remains unverified.
